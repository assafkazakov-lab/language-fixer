Language Fixer Hebrew ↔ English keyboard converter

How to use

  1. 1 Type normally. If text looks like it was typed on the wrong layout, a suggestion appears under the field.
  2. 2 Click it to convert just the affected words. Press Esc to dismiss.
  3. 3 Or press the shortcut to flip the selection — or the word at the cursor — whenever you want, including in password fields.

Keyboard shortcut

Flip language ⌥ Alt⇧ ShiftF
